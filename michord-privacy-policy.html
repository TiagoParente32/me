Privacy Policy – MiChord

Last updated: July 2026

MiChord is developed by Re:Fits. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information We Collect

MiChord uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to help us understand how the app is used and to diagnose crashes. This may include:

  • App usage events and session information
  • Device type, OS version, and app version
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • General location (country/region level only)

No personally identifiable information (name, email, etc.) is collected.

Permissions

The app may request access to MIDI and audio hardware solely to enable its core functionality (connecting and interacting with MIDI devices). No data from these sources is stored or shared.

Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics

Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
