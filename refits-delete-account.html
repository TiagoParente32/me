Account Deletion – Layer

Layer (package com.app.refits) lets you delete your account and all of its data directly from the app. No request or waiting period is needed.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open Layer
  2. Go to Profile
  3. Select Delete account
  4. Confirm with your password, or with your Google account if you signed in with Google

What Is Deleted

  • Your authentication account, including your email address and name
  • Your garments, outfits and planner entries stored in the cloud
  • Your garment images and outfit thumbnails stored in the cloud
  • Everything stored on the phone: the app returns to a fresh-install state

Data Retention

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All data is removed immediately when you confirm. No data is kept afterwards.

Using Layer Without an Account

If you never signed in, nothing about you is stored outside your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all of its data.
